Expand description

A supported topology rule with its canonical CLI and serialization name.

Variants (Non-exhaustive)§

This enum is marked as non-exhaustive
Non-exhaustive enums could have additional variants added in future. Therefore, when matching against variants of non-exhaustive enums, an extra wildcard arm must be added to account for any future variants.
§

PointMustNotOverlap

Report each coincident point-feature pair once using inclusive Euclidean distance.

§

PointMustNotOverlapWith

Report coincident point-feature pairs across two layers using inclusive Euclidean distance.

§

PointMustBeInside

Report points not strictly inside any polygon; boundary distances at or below tolerance are outside.

§

LineMustNotHaveDangles

Report part endpoints with one incident use after noding and clustering.

§

LineMustNotHavePseudoNodes

Report nodes with exactly two endpoint uses from distinct features. Closed loops are valid.

§

LineMustNotOverlap

Report shared linework between distinct features, once per feature pair. Lines meeting at an angle share none, including where one ends within tolerance of the other. Self-overlaps are excluded.

§

LineMustNotOverlapWith

Report shared linework across two layers, once per feature pair. Lines meeting at an angle share none, including where one ends within tolerance of the other.

§

LineMustNotSelfOverlap

Report linework traversed repeatedly by one feature, including its multiple parts. Parts meeting at an angle share none, including where one ends within tolerance of another.

§

LineMustNotIntersect

Report overlaps and interior-to-interior intersections between distinct features. Endpoint touches and self violations are excluded. A feature’s endpoints are where an odd number of its open parts end (OGC mod-2); dangle, pseudo-node and endpoint rules use every open part end.

§

LineMustNotIntersectWith

Report overlaps and interior-to-interior intersections across two layers. A feature’s endpoints are where an odd number of its open parts end (OGC mod-2); dangle, pseudo-node and endpoint rules use every open part end.

§

LineMustNotIntersectOrTouchInterior

Report overlaps and meetings between distinct features where at least one feature is interior. A feature’s endpoints are where an odd number of its open parts end (OGC mod-2); dangle, pseudo-node and endpoint rules use every open part end.

§

LineMustNotIntersectOrTouchInteriorWith

Report overlaps and meetings across two layers where at least one feature is interior. A feature’s endpoints are where an odd number of its open parts end (OGC mod-2); dangle, pseudo-node and endpoint rules use every open part end.

§

LineMustNotSelfIntersect

Report self-overlaps and true self-crossings. Closed loops and self-touches are valid.

§

LineMustBeInside

Report every line piece outside the polygon union and farther than tolerance from its exterior or hole boundaries. Boundary coincidence uses inclusive Euclidean distance; diagnostic pieces preserve the original source line.

§

PolygonMustNotOverlap

Report polygon intersection areas once per distinct feature pair, excluding slivers below tolerance.

§

PolygonMustNotOverlapWith

Report polygon intersection areas across two layers, excluding slivers below tolerance.

§

PolygonMustNotHaveGaps

Report union interior rings as gaps and exterior rings as outer-boundary. Ignore sliver holes below tolerance.

§

GeometryMustNotBeMultipart

Report features containing more than one part. A single-part Multi geometry is valid.

§

PointMustBeCoveredByLines

Every point must lie at Euclidean distance at most tolerance from linework, including endpoints.

§

PointMustBeCoveredByLineEndpoints

Every point must be within tolerance of an open line part endpoint. Parts whose first and last coordinates are within tolerance are closed and have no endpoints.

§

PointMustBeCoveredByPolygonBoundaries

Every point must be within tolerance of an individual polygon exterior or hole boundary, including shared edges.

§

LineEndpointsMustBeCoveredByPoints

Every open line part endpoint must be within tolerance of a point. Parts whose first and last coordinates are within tolerance are closed and have no endpoints.

§

LineMustBeCoveredByPolygonBoundaries

Every line piece must lie within inclusive Euclidean tolerance of an individual polygon exterior or hole boundary, including shared edges. Report every uncovered portion, including short tails.

§

PolygonBoundariesMustBeCoveredByLines

Every polygon exterior and hole boundary piece must lie within inclusive Euclidean tolerance of a line. Report every uncovered portion, including short tails.

§

PolygonMustContainPoint

Every polygon feature must strictly contain all retained parts of at least one point feature. Count distinct FIDs.

§

PolygonMustContainOnePoint

Every polygon feature must strictly contain exactly one point feature, and every point feature must belong to exactly one polygon feature. Count distinct FIDs.

§

LineMustNotCrossPolygonBoundaries

Report entry and exit crossings of individual exterior and hole boundaries. Tangencies, endpoint contacts and shared segments are allowed.

§

GeometryMustNotBeContainedBy

Report whole cleaned features topologically contained by individual target features, excluding mutual containment. No tolerance buffer is used.

§

PointMustBeCoveredByPolygons

Every point must be inside polygon areas or within tolerance of their boundaries.

§

PointMustNotBeInsidePolygons

Report strictly interior points. Points within tolerance of polygon boundaries are allowed.

§

PointMustNotIntersectLines

Report points at Euclidean distance at most tolerance from linework, including endpoints.

§

PointMustNotIntersectPolygons

Report points inside polygon areas or within tolerance of their boundaries.

§

LineMustBeProperlyInsidePolygons

Every cleaned line part must be inside the polygon union and farther than tolerance from its boundary. Uncovered space narrower than tolerance is covered, except between polygons that face each other only at corners. A gap widening to an open end may close up to 10% over tolerance, and parts within tolerance of a crack end or wedge tip may close. Report the whole failing part.

§

LineMustNotOverlapWithPolygons

Report line pieces inside individual polygon areas or along their boundaries, longer than tolerance. Isolated contacts are allowed.

§

LineMustNotOverlapPolygonBoundaries

Report shared individual polygon boundary segments longer than tolerance. Crossings and isolated contacts are allowed.

§

LineMustNotIntersectPolygons

Report interior pieces, shared boundary pieces and isolated contacts with individual polygons. Boundary proximity within tolerance counts as contact.

§

LineEndpointsMustBeCoveredByPolygonBoundaries

Every open line part endpoint must be within tolerance of an exterior or hole boundary. Parts whose first and last coordinates are within tolerance are closed and have no endpoints.

§

PolygonMustNotContainPoints

Report strictly contained point parts with polygon origin provenance. Points within tolerance of boundaries are allowed.
